Why Jules On The Move

A lifetime of business ownership brought Julie to real estate.

Before real estate, Julie founded and operated a specialty sandwich and spud shop, co-owned and operated a used automobile dealership, and founded and operated a nail and tanning salon. Julie also worked as a licensed and bonded automobile salesperson, licensed nail technician, and licensed auctioneer.

That background shaped a direct approach: listen carefully, work hard, solve the next problem, and stay grateful for the opportunity to help.
