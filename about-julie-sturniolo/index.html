Julie Sturniolo · San Antonio real estate

Meet Julie Sturniolo

Entrepreneur, REALTOR®, problem solver, and the person behind Jules On The Move.

Julie Sturniolo is a Texas-licensed REALTOR® serving San Antonio buyers, sellers, renters, property owners, military-connected households, 55+ clients, new-construction buyers, investors, and people planning a relocation.

An Entrepreneur Before Real Estate

Julie founded and operated a specialty sandwich and spud shop using a bread recipe Julie developed, later co-owned and operated a used automobile dealership, and founded and operated a nail and tanning salon.

A Career Built Around People

Julie has worked as a licensed and bonded automobile salesperson, licensed nail technician, salon owner, and licensed auctioneer. Each chapter required service, communication, problem-solving, and follow-through.

From Missouri to Texas

Julie relocated to Texas in January 2026 and received a Texas real estate license in July 2026. Julie is a member of the San Antonio Board of REALTORS®, Texas REALTORS®, and the National Association of REALTORS®.

Why Jules On The Move

Julie enjoys staying busy, especially when the work helps someone else. Jules On The Move reflects forward movement, useful work, and a commitment to helping clients navigate important housing decisions.
